## The Delight Test

Before publishing, ask: *Does this make the primary audience feel:*

- [ ] **Seen** — "He gets what it's actually like."
- [ ] **Smarter** — "I learned something or sharpened something I half-knew."
- [ ] **Brave** — "This gives me language to say a thing I've been afraid to say."
- [ ] **Connected** — "I want to forward this to someone specific."

If none of these are true, the content isn't ready.
